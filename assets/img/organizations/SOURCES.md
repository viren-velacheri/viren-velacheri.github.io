- `samsung-logo.png`: https://www.samsung.com/etc.clientlibs/samsung/clientlibs/consumer/global/clientlib-common/resources/images/logo-square-letter.png
- `panera-logo.svg`: https://www.panerabread.com/content/dam/panerabread/menu-omni/integrated-web/branding/panera-bread-logo-no-mother-bread.svg
- `toastmasters-logo.png`: https://www.toastmasters.org/content/images/globals/toastmasters-logo@2x.png
- `zero-robotics-logo.png`: https://d1bomxhyt3btnq.cloudfront.net/images/sponsors/ZR.png
- `learning-bridge-logo.png`: https://learningbridgetx.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/learning_bridge_color-1-e1632539894959.png

The Zero Robotics mark is current organization branding. It is used to identify the organization, not to depict the hardware used during the historical competition.
